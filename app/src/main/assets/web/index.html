Catalogs & hubs ·

Which catalogs show where on the TV, and which ones Search uses.

Hubs

Each hub is a page on the TV's menu bar, with a row for each catalog ticked in it. Home is the first page; the others are left off the menu while they have no catalogs. Move a catalog to another hub with its Hub menu.

Profiles

Each person gets their own picture, watch history, addons and catalogs, settings and MDBList account; live TV is shared. With more than one profile, the TV asks who's watching when the app starts. Choose whose things you're changing with Profile at the top of the page.

    Clean up channel names

    Comma-separated. Matching ignores case and repeats, so "UK: BBC One HD" with prefix "UK:" and suffix "HD" becomes "BBC One".

    Backup & restore

    A backup holds everything set up here: your addons and catalogs, profiles with their settings and watch history, the Xtream accounts, groups, channel edits, guides and logos. Restore it here or on another device running BingeTV Redux. A FyrTuner backup restores its live TV settings and leaves the rest as it is.
